## Full Description

Primary Play description:

Bible Inspiration helps you explore life situations through scripture-centered
AI conversation.

Ask in your own words and receive responses grounded in biblical passages,
with verse references you can review directly in the app.

Whether you are looking for comfort, reflection, or a starting point for
study, the app is designed to make scripture easier to explore from your
phone.

The Android experience is built to feel calm, readable, and focused. It also
supports multiple languages, including right-to-left support for Arabic.

Key features:

- AI-assisted conversation grounded in Bible verses
- Scripture references attached to responses
- Multilingual experience
- Fast mobile chat interface
- Designed to encourage reflection, comfort, and study

Important notes:

- Responses are generated automatically and may not always be complete or
  accurate.
- The app is intended for spiritual encouragement and informational use.
- It is not a substitute for emergency, medical, legal, or mental health care.
