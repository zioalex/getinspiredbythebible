# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-04

This Privacy Policy describes how Get Inspired by the Bible collects, uses,
and protects information when you use the website, Android application, and
related services.

This document is a repo-hosted draft intended to support Google Play Store
submission and public launch. Before publishing it as the live policy URL,
review it against the final production deployment and hosting setup.

## Who We Are

Get Inspired by the Bible is a conversational application that helps people
find spiritual encouragement and scripture relevant to their life situations.

Project contact:

- Email: [getinspiredbythebible@ai4you.sh](mailto:getinspiredbythebible@ai4you.sh)

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- The public web application
- The Android application
- Related API and support services

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

When you use the service, you may provide:

- Chat messages you submit
- Conversation history sent to support multi-turn responses
- Feedback or contact form messages, if those features are used
- Language selections or preferences you make in the app or website

### Technical Information We Collect Automatically

Depending on the client and deployment, we may collect:

- IP address
- Device and browser or app technical metadata
- User-Agent string
- Session identifiers used for service operation and usage measurement
- Language headers or locale settings
- Request timestamps and basic diagnostic logs

The current codebase also documents session tracking for analytics and service
measurement on the web experience.

### Security And Abuse-Prevention Data

We use abuse-prevention and security controls, which may include:

- Bot-detection or challenge tokens
- Rate-limiting records
- Security logs related to blocked or suspicious requests

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Provide chat responses and scripture search results
- Maintain conversation context during a session
- Operate, secure, and monitor the service
- Detect abuse, spam, or automated attacks
- Improve reliability, performance, and quality
- Respond to support and contact requests
- Measure aggregate usage of the service

## Legal Bases

If you are in a jurisdiction that requires a legal basis for processing,
our basis may include:

- Performance of the service you request when you submit a message
- Legitimate interests in securing, operating, and improving the service
- Compliance with legal obligations where applicable
- Consent where the law requires it

## Sharing Of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information with service providers and infrastructure partners
that help us operate the product, such as:

- Cloud hosting and networking providers
- Database and observability providers
- Email delivery providers for contact workflows
- Large language model providers, depending on deployment configuration

Because this project supports multiple deployment configurations, the exact
subprocessors may vary between environments.

## AI Processing

Messages you submit may be processed by AI systems to generate responses.

Depending on the deployment configuration, prompts may be processed by:

- Self-hosted models operated by the service owner
- Third-party model providers configured for the deployment

Do not submit highly sensitive personal information unless you are comfortable
with that information being processed to generate a response.

## Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate,
secure, support, and improve the service, unless a longer retention period is
required by law.

Retention periods may differ by data type, environment, and deployment setup.
Examples include:

- Operational logs retained for troubleshooting and abuse prevention
- Session records retained for aggregate usage measurement
- Contact messages retained long enough to respond and maintain support history

Before publishing this policy as the live public version, the operator should
replace this section with deployment-specific retention periods for the live
environment and verify that the public-facing policy matches the Play Store
Data safety declaration.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect
information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

These safeguards may include:

- HTTPS in production
- Rate limiting
- Content filtering and input validation
- Bot protection and abuse detection
- Restricted infrastructure access

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot
guarantee absolute security.

## International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own,
depending on where infrastructure and service providers are located.

Where required, we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are
used for such transfers.

## Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- Access personal information we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request review of a deletion request where applicable
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Request data portability where applicable
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To make a request, contact:

- [getinspiredbythebible@ai4you.sh](mailto:getinspiredbythebible@ai4you.sh)

We may need to verify your request before taking action.

Some requests may need to be handled manually and may be limited by legal,
security, operational, or technical constraints, including whether we can
reasonably associate stored information with the requesting person.

## Children's Privacy

The service is not intended for children under the age at which consent is
required in their jurisdiction without involvement from a parent or guardian.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information without appropriate
consent, contact us and we will review the request.

## Third-Party Links And Services

The service may contain links to third-party websites or rely on third-party
services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

## Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material
changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where
