# BITB-088: iOS Release Pipeline & App Store Compliance — From TestFlight to the Store

**Status:** 🎯 Todo (blocked on BITB-087)
**Priority:** P3
**Size:** L (3–5 days, plus App Review latency outside our control)
**Created:** 2026-07-29
**Part of:** the iOS delivery plan — Stage 5 of BITB-084 → BITB-085 → BITB-086 → BITB-109 → BITB-087 → **BITB-088**.

## User Story

**As an** iPhone user, **I want** to install Vox Quieta from the App Store, **so that** I can get it
the way I get every other app instead of being handed a TestFlight invite.

**As the** maintainer, **I want** iOS releases to be as automated as Android's, **so that** a second
store does not double the manual work of every release.

## Why This Is Separate From BITB-087

Android's app and Android's *release machinery* were separate efforts, and the machinery turned out
to be the fiddlier half: `.github/workflows/android-publish.yml` (tag-triggered, with a version-sanity
gate and a wait-for-green-CI gate), `android-promote.yml`, `android/fastlane/`, and a whole
Console-only compliance checklist that needed its own home in the repo
(`docs/android/play-console-compliance.md`, written precisely because that work "otherwise has no
home in the repo and is easy to lose track of"). iOS needs the same three things, and lumping them
into the app story is how they get skipped.

## Part A — Build & publish automation

Mirror the Android pipeline's shape so there is one release mental model, not two.

- **`.github/workflows/ios-publish.yml`**, tag-triggered on `v[0-9]+.[0-9]+.[0-9]+`, exactly like
  `android-publish.yml`, plus a `workflow_dispatch` with a TestFlight-vs-App-Store track input and a
  `validate` dry-run option.
- **Version comes from `.release-please-manifest.json`**, not from a hand-edited Xcode setting.
  Android already does this — `android/app/build.gradle.kts` reads the manifest and falls back to a
  literal only when it is missing. Copy that discipline: `MARKETING_VERSION` from the manifest,
  `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION` from the CI run number or an epoch timestamp (Android uses an epoch
  timestamp for `versionCode`). **Do not create `vX.Y.Z` tags by hand** — release-please owns them
  (`docs/RELEASE_PROCESS.md`), and the same tag that triggers `android-publish.yml` will now trigger
  this one.
- **Reuse the tag-gate logic** from `android-publish.yml`: assert the tag matches the manifest
  version, and wait for iOS CI on the target commit to be green before uploading. A release that
  publishes over a red build is the failure mode that gate exists to prevent.
- **fastlane** at `ios/fastlane/`, mirroring `android/fastlane/`, for build + upload. Signing per
  BITB-085's recorded strategy; if `match` is chosen, the certificate repo and its access are part of
  this story, not an afterthought.
- **Fail closed on missing secrets**, the way the Android release signing config does: non-signing
  tasks (build, test, lint) must keep working locally without any Apple credentials, and only an
  actual publish attempt may fail for missing secrets.
- **`docs/RELEASE_PROCESS.md`** updated: one tag now fans out to two stores. Say what happens if one
  succeeds and the other fails, because it will.

## Part B — Privacy & compliance (the part that gets apps rejected)

- **`PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`** privacy manifest in the app target, plus manifests from any third-party
  SDK. If BITB-087 shipped analytics as NoOp-only, this stays minimal — that is the point of having
  made that choice there.
- **App Privacy ("nutrition label") answers** in App Store Connect, derived from the **already-agreed
  Play Data safety answers** rather than re-reasoned from scratch. Per
  `docs/android/play-console-compliance.md`, the settled position is: chat message text **is shared
  with third-party AI processors** (OpenRouter, Azure OpenAI) to generate and safety-screen
  responses (the PR #900 disclosure), and **no location data is collected**. The two stores must not
  end up telling users different stories about the same backend.
- **Age rating questionnaire.** Two things to answer honestly rather than optimistically: the app
  discusses grief, violence, and death **in a biblical context** (this is the same tension BITB-020
  solved for the content filter — "David killed Goliath" must not be treated as violent content),
  and the answers are **generated by an LLM**. Apple scrutinises apps whose primary content is
  model-generated, so the review notes must state plainly that the app runs pre-generation content
  safety (Llama Guard 3 via OpenRouter, per BITB-020 / `docs/HOW-TO-ENABLE-CONTENT-SAFETY.md`), that
  `CONTENT_SAFETY_ENABLED` is on in production, and that there is no user-to-user content and no
  user-generated content surface at all.
- **Export compliance:** the app uses only standard HTTPS, so `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false`
  belongs in `Info.plist` to stop every upload asking.
- **Guideline 4.2 (minimum functionality)** pre-empted in the review notes: list the native
  capabilities — offline conversation history, native streaming chat, system-integrated
  light/dark and Dynamic Type, 11 localizations — rather than waiting for the reviewer to decide the
  app is a website.
- **No account, no IAP, no ads, no tracking, no permissions.** Say all of it in the review notes; it
  is the shortest path through review, and it is true (`AndroidManifest.xml:5-6` requests only
  `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`).
- **No donate link** — BITB-085's recorded decision. Apple's donation rules (Guideline 3.2.1(vi))
  are materially stricter than the Play carve-out BITB-074 relies on, so the Ko-fi entry point stays
  off iOS until someone deliberately revisits it.
- **`docs/ios/app-store-compliance.md`** — the running checklist of App-Store-Connect-only items,
  modelled on `docs/android/play-console-compliance.md` including its "check off and archive, don't
  delete" discipline, so future Apple policy waves have a home in the repo.

## Part C — Store listing

- **Metadata under `ios/fastlane/metadata/`**, mirroring the Android layout
  (`title.txt`, `short_description.txt`, `full_description.txt`, `changelogs/`, `images/`).
  **English-only for v1** — that is exactly what Android ships today
  (`android/fastlane/metadata/android/` contains only `en-US`), and inventing an 11-locale store
  listing for the second platform first is scope nobody asked for. The app's own 11 localizations
  still show up in App Store Connect's "Languages".
- **Screenshots** for the required iPhone display sizes. Android captured 9 screens into
  `android/play_store_assets/screenshots/` — reuse the same scene list so the two listings tell one
  story, but capture them natively (do not upscale Android frames).
- **Reuse the existing store copy** in `android/fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/` as the base,
  adjusting platform-specific wording only. Two hand-written descriptions of the same app will drift.
- **Per-release "What's New"** should come from the same changelog source the rest of the repo uses
  (`CHANGELOG.md` → `frontend/public/changelog.json` via
  `frontend/scripts/extract-latest-changelog.mjs`, and Android's `changelogs/`), not be retyped.

## Part D — Tell people it exists

- `/app` page (`frontend/src/app/[locale]/app/page.tsx`) gains the App Store badge and drops the
  iOS "Add to Home Screen" fallback from BITB-084 to a secondary position. The i18n keys
  `App.ctaButton` ("Get it on Google Play") and `App.ctaSub` ("Free on Android") are
  **Android-specific in all 11 locales** and must become platform-aware — this is 11 locales × a few
  keys, so budget for it.
- Footer, README, `docs/ROADMAP.md`, and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` updated to describe iOS as shipped.
- `docs/BACKLOG.md`: BITB-012's *Out of Scope* line "iOS app (future consideration)" is now
  historical — leave the story's text intact (it is a `Done`-adjacent record) but ensure the iOS
  stories are the live pointer.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Pushing a `vX.Y.Z` tag produces a TestFlight build with no manual Xcode step.
- [ ] The publish workflow refuses to upload when the tag and `.release-please-manifest.json`
      disagree, and when iOS CI on that commit is not green.
- [ ] A local `xcodebuild` test/build run succeeds with **no** Apple credentials present.
- [ ] `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` present and consistent with the App Privacy answers in App Store
      Connect, which are in turn consistent with the Play Data safety answers.
- [ ] `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` set; uploads stop prompting for export compliance.
- [ ] Review notes cover: no account, no IAP/ads/tracking, no permissions, LLM content-safety
      pipeline, and the Guideline 4.2 native-capability list.
- [ ] Age rating questionnaire completed, with the reasoning recorded in
      `docs/ios/app-store-compliance.md` (not just answered in the web form and forgotten).
- [ ] Store listing live: metadata, screenshots for all required iPhone sizes, support and privacy
      policy URLs pointing at `voxquieta.org`.
- [ ] **App approved and downloadable from the App Store.**
- [ ] `/app` shows the App Store badge to iOS visitors in all 11 locales, and the Play badge is
      unchanged for everyone else.
- [ ] `docs/ios/app-store-compliance.md` exists with the Console-only checklist, and
      `docs/RELEASE_PROCESS.md` documents the two-store tag fan-out.
- [ ] A rejection, if it happens, is recorded in the compliance doc with what changed — the paper
      trail matters more than the pass.

## Out of Scope

- App Store **Search Ads** or any paid promotion.
- Localizing the store listing beyond English (matches the current Android listing).
- Automated screenshot generation (fastlane `snapshot`) — nice, but not before the first release.
- Tier 2 app features from BITB-087.
- Promotion automation between TestFlight and production. Android has `android-promote.yml`; add the
  iOS analogue once there is a release cadence to automate.
