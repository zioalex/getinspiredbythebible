**Status:** 🎯 Todo
**Size:** S

Deferred from BITB-198 verification (product decision). With the new weapon nouns, `Хочу умереть. Застрелюсь из пистолета`, `我想用手枪自杀`, `죽고 싶어. 권총으로 자살하고 싶어`, `मैं मरना चाहता हूँ, पिस्तौल से` take the ambiguous self-harm + violence route (crisis block). This is the same behavior as English today (`I want to kill myself with a gun`). Decide whether a weapon noun as a method should stay ambiguous or be treated as compassionate.
