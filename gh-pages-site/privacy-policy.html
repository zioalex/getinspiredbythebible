Who We Are

Get Inspired by the Bible is a conversational application that helps people find spiritual encouragement and scripture relevant to their life situations.

Contact: getinspiredbythebible@ai4you.sh

Information We Collect

  • Messages you submit and related context for response generation
  • Technical request metadata such as IP, user agent, locale
  • Security and abuse-prevention signals
  • Operational logs and session-level usage information

How We Use Information

  • Provide chat and scripture-related responses
  • Operate, secure, monitor, and improve the service
  • Detect abuse and protect service availability
  • Respond to support and contact requests

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data may be processed through infrastructure and service providers needed to run the platform.

AI Processing

Submitted messages may be processed by AI systems configured for the deployment. Avoid sharing highly sensitive personal data.

Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary for operational, security, support, and legal requirements. Retention may vary by environment and data type.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction, restriction, portability, or consent withdrawal where applicable. Requests can be sent to the contact email.

Security

We apply safeguards such as HTTPS, rate limiting, content filtering, and abuse detection, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

This policy may be updated over time. Material changes are reflected by an updated date.
